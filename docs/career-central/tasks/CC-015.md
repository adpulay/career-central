# CC-015 — Reliable discovery and explainable shortlist

Status: proposed next implementation, not started. Product milestone P1. Requirements R02/R03/R09/R17/R18; features F01–F07 and the matching prerequisite of F55–F59. This specification grants no recurring monitoring or application actions.

## Small useful outcome

A user opens one shortlist and can decide which opportunity to review next, knowing why it matched, which eligibility facts are unknown, when/how it was sourced, and whether related applications already exist. Establish only the shared policy/identity slice needed for this and the early priority monitor; do not build a general matching platform first.

Reuse scan.mjs/providers, lib/admission-gate.mjs, URL/requisition identity helpers, lib/applications/discovery.mjs, scripts/discover-ranked.mjs, source-backed evaluation reports and the existing canonical read projection. Inspect before choosing the smallest adapter/refactor. Preserve existing canonical file/ledger owners and historical scores.

## Scope and acceptance

1. Compare mature scanner and ranked extension admission/identity behavior on fictional postings. Build a reviewed corpus containing eligible, excluded and unknown cases: graduation/start, remote/geography, work authorization versus clearance, experience alternatives, compensation missing/qualified, duplicate/related/reposted requisitions, incomplete descriptions and stale/expired listings.
2. Read targeting/role-family weights/skills from verified user-layer configuration; do not add user-specific constants to system code. Keep a heuristic score distinct from source-backed AI evaluation and missing evidence. A match score is not a hiring probability.
3. Produce a bounded stable shortlist with company/role/location, salary components or unknowns, publication provenance and first-seen separately, source freshness/failures, match method/version, essential/preferred evidence, gaps, exclusions/unknowns and prior-application conflict hints. Retain separate requisitions and explicit incomplete coverage.
4. Link to existing job/application detail when exact identity is available; use a minimal existing UI/CLI entry point rather than another engine/database. Preserve old consumers or version the contract deliberately.
5. Freeze fixture expectations and record false admissions/exclusions and ranking disagreements explicitly; all designated hard-ineligible fixtures must stay excluded, all incomplete eligibility fixtures remain reviewable, and exact duplicates cannot consume multiple shortlist slots. Use reviewed order constraints rather than claims of statistically calibrated accuracy.
6. Run meaningful scanner/admission/ranking/identity/projection regressions on fictional data; verify no browser, auth, private-record mutation or submission in fixture mode. No paid dependency.
7. Later live acceptance: explicitly scoped sources/request budget and user review of a small sample, with provenance and source failures shown. Do not equate offline acceptance with live quality or activate schedules as a test side effect.

Deliver a reusable eligibility/identity/explanation boundary for [CC-016](CC-016.md). Defer general company enrichment, more ATS submit transports, autonomous actions, generic background queues and AWS. Completion requires the useful bounded shortlist and evidence above, not just a new schema or provider count.
